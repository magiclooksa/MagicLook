Patterned brand ground — use as the outer field of posters, story frames, stationery backs and hero areas; keep text in a clear (unpatterned) card on top.
```jsx
<Pattern ground="ivory" scale={120} style={{padding:40}}>
  <Card>…</Card>
</Pattern>
```
- `ground`: ivory (13%) / ink (10%) / ochre / white. Never stretch: `scale` is uniform.
