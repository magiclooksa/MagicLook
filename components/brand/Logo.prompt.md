Brand signature — use wherever the Magic Look identity appears (headers, posters, stationery); never redraw or restyle the mark.
```jsx
<Logo layout="horizontal" size={64} />
<Logo layout="stacked" tone="dark" />
<LogoMark size={32} color="ochre" />
```
- `layout`: horizontal | stacked | mark. Use `mark` alone below 25mm / ~90px signature width.
- `tone`: light (ivory grounds), dark (ink/ochre grounds, ivory mark), mono.
- Keep clear space of one stem width around it; never place over busy photography without a calm field.
